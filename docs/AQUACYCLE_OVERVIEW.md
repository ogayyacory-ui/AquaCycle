# AquaCycle: Water and Sanitation Action

## About the app

AquaCycle is a community-focused water sustainability app. It brings together
information, local reporting, and practical ideas to help people understand
water challenges and take action in their communities.

Water and sanitation problems are often difficult to address when leaks,
pollution, drainage issues, and unsafe facilities go unnoticed or when people
do not know what solutions are available. AquaCycle is designed to make it
easier to share information about these problems and encourage coordinated,
locally relevant responses.

## How AquaCycle can help

### Report local water problems

People can submit reports about water-related issues, including a problem type,
location, description, urgency, and optional supporting media. Reports can
include GPS coordinates when available. This helps create a record of reported
issues that communities and responsible organizations can review and use to
prioritize follow-up.

Examples of issues that communities may report include leaks, drainage
problems, pollution, water-access concerns, or sanitation needs.

### Understand local weather

With the phone owner's permission, the mobile app can use the device's current
location to show local weather and display the coordinates on a map. Local
weather information can help people consider rainfall, plan water collection,
and prepare for conditions that may affect drainage or water quality. Location
access is used for this feature only when granted by the user.

### Explore practical water and sanitation solutions

AquaCycle presents ideas such as rainwater harvesting, greywater reuse,
bio-sand filtration, smart water monitoring, and constructed wetlands.
Explaining possible approaches can help households and communities consider
options that fit their local needs and resources. Treatment and reuse must
always be appropriate to the water source and intended use; app information is
not a substitute for professional water-quality testing or public-health
guidance.

### Support community participation

The app includes community events and user accounts to support participation
and coordination. Events can provide a way to bring people together for
awareness, cleanup, maintenance, or other locally organized water and
sanitation activities.

### Compare potential interventions

The community simulator lets users explore example systems, including
rainwater tanks, water-treatment systems, eco-toilets, recycling stations,
sustainable drainage, wetlands, solar pumps, and water sensors. It can help
people compare potential interventions and discuss their expected benefits.
Its estimates are planning illustrations, not measured or guaranteed outcomes.

### Make reported needs visible

The impact dashboard brings together community report and event counts. Some
other displayed impact figures are illustrative demo values, not verified
measurements of AquaCycle's real-world impact. This distinction should remain
clear when using the dashboard for planning or public communication.

## Intended impact

By making it easier to report issues, find local information, learn about
solutions, and organize community participation, AquaCycle aims to help:

- surface water and sanitation concerns that might otherwise go unreported;
- support better-informed local discussions and follow-up;
- encourage water conservation, rainwater collection, and suitable reuse;
- connect communities with practical sanitation and wastewater-management
  ideas; and
- promote care for rivers, wetlands, drainage systems, and other shared
  resources.

The app is a tool for awareness and coordination. Lasting improvements depend
on accurate reports, community involvement, qualified technical advice, and
action from the organizations responsible for water and sanitation services.
